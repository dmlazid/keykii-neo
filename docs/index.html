KeyKii Neo Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

KeyKii Neo is an Android keyboard and customization app. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the keyboard, translation and grammar tools, voice input, themes, fonts, and advertising features.

Typing and keyboard input

Normal keyboard typing is processed on the device so KeyKii can enter the characters you choose into the active app. KeyKii does not intentionally send ordinary typed text to our servers. Clipboard history, emoji recents, text shortcuts, keyboard preferences, selected themes, selected fonts, and the 24-hour rewarded-access expiry are stored locally on your device unless a feature described below requires network access.

Translation and grammar tools

For supported languages, translation uses Google ML Kit on-device translation. A Cebuano translation fallback may use an online service only when you actively tap Translate. Grammar Fix sends only the text placed in its grammar box to the LanguageTool service when you actively tap Check grammar. These network tools are optional and are not used for ordinary typing.

Voice input

KeyKii requests microphone access for voice-typing features. Microphone access is used only when you start a voice-input action and is subject to Android permission controls.

Advertising

KeyKii uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform. Ads may include rewarded ads that provide 24-hour access to eligible themes and fonts, plus occasional interstitial ads shown only at natural breaks inside the KeyKii settings/shop app. Ads are not shown inside the keyboard while you type.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, diagnostic/performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID. Google states that this data is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and is encrypted in transit.

Consent and ad privacy controls

Where required, KeyKii uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent or present privacy choices before requesting ads. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point for your region, KeyKii exposes it in the app's Privacy screen.

Incognito mode

KeyKii includes an Incognito mode that pauses new clipboard-history and emoji-recent saves. Existing pinned clips, favorites, shortcuts, and preferences remain stored locally until you remove or reset them.

Data retention and deletion

Local KeyKii preferences remain on the device until you change them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Third-party services may retain information according to their own privacy policies and legal requirements.

Children

KeyKii is not designed specifically for children. The Play Store target-audience declaration should reflect the actual audience selected by the developer before release.

Third-party services

KeyKii currently integrates Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform, Google ML Kit, and LanguageTool for the optional features described above. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when KeyKii adds or changes features, SDKs, data practices, or legal requirements. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
